Stream & earn

Go live in a channel you own, keep a recording, and get paid by the people watching.

Go live

  1. Open a channel you own. No channel yet? Tap +, then Create channel.
  2. Tap Go Live. Choose your camera or your screen, then tap Start broadcast.
  3. That’s it. Everyone in the room sees a LIVE card and can join with one tap.

Record your stream

Turn on Record when you go live. When you finish, a past broadcast card appears in the room so people can watch it later. It takes about 30 seconds to be ready.

Get paid

Tips with Lightning

Add your Lightning Address (for example you@getalby.com) in your profile or in Creator Studio. Viewers tap Tip and the money goes straight to your wallet in seconds. The server never holds it.

Cards and subscriptions

If the server owner has turned on card payments (Stripe), you can also take card tips and sell subscription tiers. Subscribers can get extras such as subscriber-only recordings.

Creator Studio

Your earnings, viewer numbers, subscription tiers and profile, all in one place. It’s in the app, and on the web at /_mm/dashboard/ (sign in with your Matrix account).

Turn streaming off in a room

Some rooms are just for chat. Open Channel settings and switch MatrixMedia off. The Go Live, Tip and LIVE buttons disappear for everyone in that room. Switch it back on any time.

Viewers from other servers. Matrix is federated: people with accounts on other Matrix servers can join your room and watch too.
