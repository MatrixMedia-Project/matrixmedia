Watch & tip

Watch live streams right in the chat, and support the people you watch.

Watch a live stream

When someone in a room goes live, a LIVE card appears in the chat. Tap it and the stream plays. A few channels show a short ad first. Most show none.

Send a tip

Tap Tip while you watch and choose an amount.

  • Lightning (fastest). Pay from any Lightning wallet, such as Phoenix, Wallet of Satoshi or Alby. The creator gets it in seconds, with almost no fees.
  • One-tap tips. Tap Pair a wallet once (Nostr Wallet Connect). After that, every tip is a single tap.
  • Card. If the creator accepts cards, you’ll also see a card option. No account needed.

Watch past broadcasts

Recorded streams stay in the room as past broadcast cards. Tap one to watch. Cards marked Subscribers only need a subscription.

Subscribe

Some creators sell subscriptions. Tap Subscribe to see their tiers. What you get (for example subscriber-only recordings) depends on the creator.
