Quick start

Live streaming inside Matrix chat rooms. Pick what you want to do.

Watch & tip →Join live streams and support creators. Stream & earn →Go live in your own channel and get paid. Run your own server →One command, your domain, your rules.

Start in three steps

  1. Get the app. It’s free on iPhone and Android. No phone? Use it in your browser.

    App Store Google Play Open in browser

  2. Create an account. Open the app and tap Create account. Pick a username and a password and accept the terms. No email needed.

    If the app asks for a server, enter matrix.steegler.com. Your address will look like @yourname:steegler.com.

  3. Watch or go live. When someone is streaming, a LIVE card appears in the room. Tap it to watch. To stream yourself, open a channel you own and tap Go Live.

Want to get tips?

Add a Lightning Address to your profile. It looks like an email, for example you@getalby.com. Tips then go straight to your wallet. The server never holds your money.

No wallet yet? Alby and Wallet of Satoshi give you a Lightning Address in a couple of minutes.

This is a test server. matrix.steegler.com runs the newest MatrixMedia code, so features change often. Found a bug? Tell us on GitHub.
