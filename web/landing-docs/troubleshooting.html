Help

Quick fixes for the problems people hit most.

I can’t sign in

Check the server. On this site it’s matrix.steegler.com, and your username looks like @yourname:steegler.com. Accounts from other Matrix servers sign in on their own server, not here.

I can’t create an account

  • Username taken: pick another one.
  • Asked to wait: sign-ups are rate-limited to stop spam. Try again later.

My stream won’t start

  1. Allow camera and microphone access. On a phone: system Settings → MatrixMedia. In a browser: click the lock icon next to the address.
  2. Sharing your screen? Make sure you picked a screen or window in the dialog.
  3. Try another network. Some office and school networks block live video.

Viewers see a black screen

This is almost always the viewer’s network. Ask them to reopen the stream, or to switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.

My tip didn’t go through

  • The invoice expired. Tap Tip again to get a fresh one.
  • The amount was too small. Some wallets refuse tiny payments. Try 100 sats or more.
  • Your wallet says “paid” but the tip isn’t confirmed. Copy the payment proof (the preimage) from your wallet and paste it into the tip screen. The server checks it and marks the tip as paid.

A recording is missing

Recordings take about 30 seconds to be ready after a stream ends. Reopen the room. If there’s still no past broadcast card after a minute, report it (see below) with the channel name and roughly when the stream ended.

Someone on another Matrix server can’t watch

Make sure MatrixMedia is switched on for the room (Channel settings → MatrixMedia) and that they have joined the room. Then ask them to reopen it.

Running your own server?

Run sudo mmctl doctor on the server. It re-runs the installer’s checks and tells you what’s wrong. More fixes are in the install guide.

Still stuck?

Open an issue on GitHub. Tell us what you tried and what you saw, your app or browser, your phone or computer, and roughly when it happened.
